# Dew Tracker privacy policy

*Last updated: October 13, 2026*

Dew Tracker is an iPhone app made by Old Gaffer Software ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with information about you. In short: **we don't collect any personal information.** What you record stays on your iPhone.

## What stays on your iPhone

The flavours you mark as tried, your ratings, favourites and notes, the date you logged each one, and (if you choose) the city where you tried it are stored only on your iPhone. We have no servers that receive this information, and we can't see it. There is no account or sign-in. Deleting the app deletes this information, apart from any copy in your own iPhone backups.

## Location (optional)

The first time you log a flavour, the app asks whether you'd like to save the city where you tried it. If you say yes and allow location access:

- The app asks iOS for an approximate location (to about a kilometre) only at the moment you log a flavour.
- iOS's geocoding service, provided by Apple, turns that location into a city and region name, such as "Winnipeg, Manitoba". Apple handles this under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- Only the city and region name are saved, on your iPhone. Coordinates are never stored and never sent to us.

You can turn this off at any time in the app under **Settings → Location**, or turn off location access for Dew Tracker in your iPhone's Settings.

## Flavour list updates

To check for a newer flavour list, the app downloads two public files from GitHub (owned by GitHub, Inc.). The app sends nothing about you or your list. Like any website, GitHub's servers receive standard connection information, such as your IP address, to deliver the files. GitHub handles that under the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). You can turn off automatic checks in the app under **Settings → Flavour updates**.

## What we don't do

- No analytics, advertising or tracking
- No third-party SDKs that collect data
- No selling or sharing of personal information

## Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If the app's handling of information changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
