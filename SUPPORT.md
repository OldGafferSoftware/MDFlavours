# Dew Tracker support

Dew Tracker is an iPhone app for keeping track of the soda flavours you've tried: rate them, mark favourites, add notes, and see which flavours are on sale where.

## Get help

- **Email:** [info@oldgaffersoftware.com](mailto:info@oldgaffersoftware.com)
- **Report a problem or a flavour correction:** [open an issue](https://github.com/OldGafferSoftware/MDFlavours/issues/new) (needs a free GitHub account)

When reporting a problem, it helps to include your iPhone model, iOS version, and the Dew Tracker version (Settings in the app, or the App Store page).

## Common questions

**A flavour is missing, or its details are wrong.**
The flavour list is checked and updated daily. If something is still missing or wrong after a few days, email us or open an issue with a link to a news report or official announcement.

**How do I get the latest flavour list?**
Open the app with an internet connection. When a newer list is available, an **Update** banner appears on the Flavours tab. You can also go to **Settings → Flavour updates → Check now**. Your tried flavours, ratings and notes are kept when the list updates.

**Why don't upcoming flavours appear?**
The app lists flavours that have actually been released somewhere. Upcoming and rumoured flavours are added once they're on sale.

**How do I stop the app saving where I tried a flavour?**
Go to **Settings → Location** and turn off **Save city when I log a flavour**.

**Does the app need an account?**
No. Everything you record stays on your iPhone. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).

**Is my list backed up?**
Your list is stored on your iPhone and included in your normal iPhone backups (iCloud or computer). There's no separate Dew Tracker account or sync yet.
